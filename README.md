# E-commerce Sales & Profit Strategy Dashboard 📈🛒

## 📌 Project Overview
This project transforms 300+ raw transactional records into a strategic Business Intelligence tool. The focus was to move beyond simple sales tracking and deep-dive into **Profitability Analysis**, identifying how discounts and regional demographics affect the company's bottom line.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
- **Power BI:** Advanced data visualization and dashboard interactivity.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Engineered custom measures for:
  - **Profit Margin %:** Tracking real-time profitability per category.
  - **Total Orders (345):** Measuring transaction velocity.
  - **Unit Price Impact:** Analyzing price sensitivity across categories.
- **Data Modeling:** Implementation of a Star Schema for optimized cross-filtering between Product, Region, and Time.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Discoveries
- **The Profit Gap:** While "Electronics" leads in sales volume, "Office Supplies" often yields a higher net profit margin due to lower shipping overhead.
- **Regional Efficiency:** The **South Region** maintains the most stable profit margin, suggesting it as the ideal location for testing new high-margin product lines.
- **Discount Fatigue:** Identified specific price points where increasing discounts stopped driving additional volume and began eroding profit.
